# Saving a little each week

**Saving** means keeping some money for later instead of spending all of it.

Small amounts grow: 200 RWF every week for 10 weeks is **2,000 RWF**.

## How to start

- Choose a goal, for example school fees or a small business.
- Decide an amount you can keep every week.
- Keep it in a safe place.
- Write each saving in a notebook.

## Needs and wants

A **need** is something you must have, like food. A **want** is something nice to have, like sweets.
