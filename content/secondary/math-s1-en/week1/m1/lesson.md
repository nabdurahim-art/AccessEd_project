# Lines and types of angles

## Think first

Look at the edge of your desk. Does it go on forever, or does it have two ends?

## Points and lines

- A **point** marks an exact position. It has no size. We name it with a capital letter, such as A.
- A **line** is straight and goes on forever in **both** directions.
- A **line segment** is a part of a line with **two endpoints**. You can measure it. The edge of your desk is a line segment.
- A **ray** has **one endpoint** and goes on forever in **one** direction. Think of the light from a torch.

Two special pairs of lines:

- **Parallel lines** are always the same distance apart and never meet.
- **Perpendicular lines** meet at 90°.

## Angles

An **angle** is formed when two rays or lines meet at a point. The point is called the **vertex**. We measure angles in degrees.

## Types of angles

- An **acute angle** is less than 90°.
- A **right angle** is exactly 90°.
- An **obtuse angle** is more than 90° and less than 180°.
- A **straight angle** is exactly 180°.
- A **reflex angle** is more than 180° and less than 360°.
- A **full turn** is 360°.

You see these every day. A door open a little makes an acute angle. A door pushed wide open makes an obtuse angle. A flat road is a straight angle.

## Example

Name the type of each angle: 135° and 200°.

- 135° is more than 90° and less than 180°, so it is **obtuse**.
- 200° is more than 180° and less than 360°, so it is **reflex**.
