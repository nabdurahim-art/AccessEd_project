# Week 2 recap: statistics with ungrouped data

## The three big ideas

**Idea one: organising data.** Data is qualitative or quantitative, discrete or continuous. A frequency table shows how often each value appears. Cumulative frequency is a running total.

**Idea two: the mean.** Add all the values and divide by how many there are. From a table, multiply each value by its frequency first and divide by the total frequency.

**Idea three: median and mode.** The median is the middle value of the ordered data. The mode is the most common value.

## Real-life challenge: the class test

Nine students took a test marked out of 20. Their marks were:

12, 15, 9, 15, 18, 10, 15, 14, 9

Find these before you read on.

- The mean mark
- The median mark
- The mode
- A student says: "Most of us scored above the mean." Is that true?

## Check your work

- Sum: 12 + 15 + 9 + 15 + 18 + 10 + 15 + 14 + 9 = 117. Mean: 117 ÷ 9 = 13.
- In order: 9, 9, 10, 12, 14, 15, 15, 15, 18. The fifth value is the median: 14.
- The mode is 15. It appears three times.
- Five students scored above 13: the marks 14, 15, 15, 15 and 18. Five out of nine is more than half, so the student is right.
