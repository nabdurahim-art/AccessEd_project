# The five kingdoms

## Think first

A mushroom grows from the ground like a plant. But is it green? Does it make its own food?

## Five kingdoms

All organisms are placed in five big groups called **kingdoms**: Animalia, Plantae, Fungi, Protoctista and Monera.

Three features are used to decide the kingdom: the **structure of the body**, the way the organism **gets its food**, and the way it **reproduces**.

## Animalia (animals)

- Made of many cells. The cells have **no cell wall**.
- They feed on other organisms. We say they are heterotrophic.
- Most can move from place to place.
- Examples: snail, mosquito, snake, bird, human.

## Plantae (plants)

- Made of many cells. The cells have a **cell wall**.
- They contain a green substance called **chlorophyll** and make their own food by **photosynthesis**.
- Examples: moss, fern, maize, avocado tree.

## Fungi

- They do **not** photosynthesise. They take food from other living or dead material.
- They reproduce by **spores**, not seeds.
- Their cells have a cell wall made of a substance called chitin.
- Yeast is a single cell. Moulds and mushrooms have many cells.

## Protoctista

- Single cells, or groups of single cells living together.
- Each cell has a **true nucleus** with a membrane around it.
- Examples: amoeba, paramecium.

## Monera

- Single cells with **no true nucleus**. There is no membrane around the genetic material.
- They are the smallest and simplest organisms.
- Example: bacteria.

## Example

To which kingdom does a mushroom belong?

- It is not green and does not photosynthesise, so it is not a plant.
- It reproduces by spores.
- It belongs to the kingdom **Fungi**.

## Try it

- An organism has cell walls and chlorophyll. Which kingdom?
- An organism is a single cell with no true nucleus. Which kingdom?

Check your answers: Plantae, then Monera.
