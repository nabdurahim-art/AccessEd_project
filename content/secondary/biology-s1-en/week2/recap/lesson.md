# Week 2 recap: plant and animal cells

## The three big ideas

**Idea one: the cell.** The cell is the basic unit of life. Unicellular organisms have one cell and multicellular organisms have many. We need a microscope to see cells.

**Idea two: the parts of a cell.** The cell membrane controls what goes in and out. The cytoplasm is where reactions take place. The nucleus is the control centre. The mitochondria release energy. Vacuoles store fluid.

**Idea three: plant cells and animal cells.** Only plant cells have a cell wall, chloroplasts and a large central vacuole. Animal cells vary in shape and have small vacuoles or none.

## Real-life challenge: two slides

A learner looks at two slides under a light microscope.

- Slide A shows cells like neat boxes in rows. Each has a thick outline and a large clear space in the middle. The nucleus is at the side.
- Slide B shows rounded, uneven cells. Each has a thin outline and a nucleus near the centre.

One slide is onion skin and the other is cells from the inside of a cheek. Answer these before you read on.

- Which slide is the onion and which is the cheek?
- What is the thick outline in slide A, and what is the large clear space?
- Which organelle in both kinds of cell releases energy?
- The cheek cells and the onion cells are both from multicellular organisms. What does that mean?
