# Using money and change

When you buy something, you pay the price. If you give more than the price, the seller gives you **change**.

## Total cost

A notebook costs 400 RWF and a pen costs 250 RWF.

400 + 250 = **650 RWF**

## Change

You pay with a 1,000 RWF note for something that costs 700 RWF.

1,000 - 700 = **300 RWF change**

## Check your change

Always count your change before you walk away.
