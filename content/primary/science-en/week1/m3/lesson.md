# Plants and what they need

Plants are living things. To grow well, a plant needs:

- **water**
- **sunlight**
- **air**
- **good soil**

## Parts of a plant

- The **roots** hold the plant in the ground and take in water.
- The **stem** carries water up to the leaves.
- The **leaves** use sunlight to make food for the plant.
- The **flower** makes seeds and fruit.

Try it: put a bean seed in wet soil and watch it grow for a week.
