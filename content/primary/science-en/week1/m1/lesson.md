# Living and non-living things

**Living things** are alive. People, goats, birds and trees are living things.

A living thing can:

- grow
- need food and water
- have babies or make seeds

**Non-living things** are not alive. A stone, a chair, a bicycle and a cup are non-living.

A tree does not walk, but it grows and makes seeds. So a tree is living.

Try it: name three living things and three non-living things near you.
