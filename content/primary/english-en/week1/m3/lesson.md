# Simple sentences

A **sentence** tells a whole idea. It starts with a **capital letter** and ends with a **full stop**.

## I am, you are, he or she is

- I **am** a student.
- You **are** my friend.
- She **is** my sister.
- He **is** a farmer.

## I have, she has

- I **have** a book.
- She **has** a bag.

Try it: write three sentences about yourself.
