# Greetings and my name

## Say hello

- **Good morning** (in the morning)
- **Good afternoon** (after midday)
- **Good evening** (when the sun goes down)
- **Goodbye** (when you leave)

## Talk about yourself

- Hello, my name is **Amina**.
- What is your name?
- How are you?
- I am fine, thank you.
- Nice to meet you.

Try it: say hello and tell a friend your name in English.
