# Colours and everyday things

## Colours

red, blue, green, yellow, black, white

## Things we use

- a book
- a pen
- a bag
- a table
- a chair
- a door

## Put them together

- This is a **red** bag.
- That is a **blue** pen.
- I have a **green** book.

Try it: say the colour of three things near you.
