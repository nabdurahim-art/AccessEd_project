# My family

A family is a group of people who care for each other. Families can be big or small.

## Who is in a family?

- mother and father
- brother and sister
- grandmother and grandfather
- aunt and uncle
- cousin

Your father's brother is your **uncle**. Your mother's mother is your **grandmother**.

## How we help at home

- Fetch water and sweep the yard.
- Look after younger children.
- Greet older people politely. This shows **respect**.

Try it: say the name of each person in your family.
