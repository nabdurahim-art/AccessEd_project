# Rwanda, our country

Rwanda is a country in Africa. Its capital city is **Kigali**.

## Where we live

Rwanda is divided into provinces and districts. **Bugesera** is a district in the Eastern Province.

## The flag

The flag of Rwanda has the colours **blue**, **yellow** and **green**, and a golden sun.

## Our languages

Kinyarwanda is spoken all over Rwanda. English and French are also used in schools and offices.
