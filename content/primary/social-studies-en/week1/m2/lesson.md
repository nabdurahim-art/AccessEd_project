# Our community

A **community** is the people who live near each other, like a village or a neighbourhood.

## People who help us

- A **teacher** helps us learn.
- A **nurse** looks after sick people at the health centre.
- A **farmer** grows food.
- A **driver** carries people and goods.
- A **shopkeeper** sells things we need.

We need each other. When people work together, the community is strong.

## Umuganda

In Rwanda, people do **umuganda** on the last Saturday of every month. They work together to clean roads, build and help their neighbours.

Try it: who is a helper in your community?
