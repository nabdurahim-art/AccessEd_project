# Adding and taking away

**Adding** means putting numbers together. The sign is **+**.

**Taking away** means removing some. The sign is **-**.

## Adding

- 3 + 2 = **5**
- Three mangoes and two mangoes make five mangoes.

## Taking away

- 5 - 2 = **3**
- You have five eggs. You use two. Three eggs are left.

You can use your fingers, small stones or sticks to help you.

Try it: what is 4 + 3?
