# Week 1 recap: past and future

## The three big ideas

**Idea one: regular verbs in the past.** For things that are finished, add -ed: played, helped, cleaned. After did or did not, use the plain verb.

**Idea two: irregular verbs and connectors.** Some verbs change in their own way: went, met, got, ate, came. Use was with I, he, she and it. Use were with you, we and they. First, Then, After that and Finally show the order.

**Idea three: future plans.** Use am, is or are going to for a plan. Use want to and would like to for wishes. After to, use the plain verb.

## Real-life challenge: a letter to a friend

Complete this letter. Put each verb in brackets in the correct form.

Dear Keza,

Last Sunday I (visit) my grandmother. First, we (go) to her garden. Then, we (eat) lunch together. I (be) very happy. Next week I am going to (help) her again. One day I would like to (build) a new kitchen for her.

Your friend, Mutoni

Work it out before you read on.

## Check your work

- visit becomes **visited** (regular, past)
- go becomes **went** (irregular, past)
- eat becomes **ate** (irregular, past)
- be becomes **was** (with I, past)
- **help** stays plain after going to
- **build** stays plain after would like to

You used all three lessons in one letter. Well done for trying.
