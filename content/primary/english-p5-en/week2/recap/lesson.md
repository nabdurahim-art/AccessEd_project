# Week 2 recap: comparing things

## The three big ideas

**Idea one: -er than.** To compare two things with a short adjective, add -er and use than: taller than, bigger than, heavier than.

**Idea two: more, better, worse.** Long adjectives take more: more expensive than. Five adjectives are irregular: better, worse, farther, more, less.

**Idea three: the -est and the most.** To compare one thing with all the others, use the superlative: the tallest, the most beautiful, the best.

## Real-life challenge: going to town

Three ways to get from the village to town:

- Walking costs 0 Rwf and takes 3 hours.
- The bus costs 1,000 Rwf and takes 1 hour.
- The motorbike taxi costs 2,500 Rwf and takes 40 minutes.

Complete these sentences before you read on.

- The bus is (cheap) than the motorbike taxi.
- The motorbike taxi is (expensive) than the bus.
- The motorbike taxi is (fast) of the three.
- Walking is (cheap) of the three.
- For a person with a heavy bag, the bus is (good) than walking.

## Check your work

- The bus is **cheaper than** the motorbike taxi.
- The motorbike taxi is **more expensive than** the bus.
- The motorbike taxi is **the fastest** of the three.
- Walking is **the cheapest** of the three.
- The bus is **better than** walking.
