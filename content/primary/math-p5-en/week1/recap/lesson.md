# Week 1 recap: angles and lines

## The three big ideas

**Idea one: bisecting.** To bisect an angle is to cut it into two equal parts. Bisect 90° and you get 45°. Bisect 60° and you get 30°.

**Idea two: where lines cross.** Angles on a straight line add up to 180°. Angles round a point add up to 360°. Opposite angles are equal. Perpendicular lines meet at 90°.

**Idea three: parallel lines and triangles.** Corresponding angles are equal. Alternate angles are equal. Co-interior angles add up to 180°. The three angles of a triangle add up to 180°.

## Real-life challenge: the roof frame

A carpenter makes a roof frame in the shape of a triangle. The two bottom angles are 40° each.

- First, find the angle at the top of the frame.
- Then, she fixes a support beam from the top down to the floor beam. The support bisects the top angle. How big is each half?
- Last, the support meets the floor beam and makes an angle of 90° on the left side. What is the angle on the right side?

Work it out before you read on.

## Check your work

- Top angle: 40 + 40 = 80, and 180 - 80 = 100°.
- Each half: 100 ÷ 2 = 50°.
- Right side: the floor beam is a straight line, so 180 - 90 = 90°.

You used all three lessons in one problem. Well done for trying.
