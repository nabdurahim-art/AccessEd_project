# Adding and subtracting

**Adding** puts amounts together. **Subtracting** takes an amount away.

## Adding

25 + 14 = 39

- Add the ones: 5 + 4 = 9
- Add the tens: 2 + 1 = 3
- Answer: 39

## Subtracting

38 - 12 = 26

- Subtract the ones: 8 - 2 = 6
- Subtract the tens: 3 - 1 = 2
- Answer: 26

## With money

You have 300 RWF and earn 200 RWF: 300 + 200 = 500 RWF.

You have 500 RWF and spend 150 RWF: 500 - 150 = 350 RWF.
