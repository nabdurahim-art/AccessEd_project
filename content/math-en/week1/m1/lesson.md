# Counting and place value

A number is made of digits. Each digit has a place.

In **47** there are **4 tens** and **7 ones**. So 47 = 40 + 7.

- 23 = 2 tens and 3 ones
- 85 = 8 tens and 5 ones
- 100 = ten tens

## Counting in tens

10, 20, 30, 40, 50 ...

Try it: how many tens and ones are in 62?
